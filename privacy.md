## Privacy Policy

### Scope

This policy applies solely to the browser extension developed by us. (hereinafter referred to as @tkhm)

### Basic Policy

We collect only the necessary and minimal information required for the functionality of this extension.
Specifically, this extension collects the URL and title of the websites visited by the user when the user utilizes the extension's features.

### Retention Period

The information collected by the extension is not controlled by the developer and relies on the clipboard functionality of the PC on which the extension is used. The information is stored only as long as it remains in the clipboard.

### Provision to Third Parties

The information collected by this extension is not transmitted externally by the developer and is not provided to any third parties.

### Contact Information

For any inquiries regarding this privacy policy, please contact us.
